**2026-01-07–13 — 🌡️🧊 First-half January thaws**
   - DNR documents two thaws in the first half of January with overnight temperatures remaining below freezing. This comparison window follows the January 5–6 rain-on-snow event.
   - Sources: [Primary record / report](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/hydroclim/hydroclim-minnesota-early-february-2026.html)

**2026-01-22–25 — 🥶 Extended deep freeze**
   - Twin Cities temperatures remained at or below 0°F for 68 hours, from January 22 at 4 p.m. to January 25 at noon.
   - Sources: [Primary record / report](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/climate/journal/zero_and_below_streaks.html)
